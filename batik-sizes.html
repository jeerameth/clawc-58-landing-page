Please Note

  • Size is measured in cm for Body Length, Shoulder Width (seam to seam) and Body Width.
  • All batik shirts follow UNIQLO Malaysia standard Asian sizing, which typically runs one full size smaller than US or UK Western sizing. This means if you normally wear a Western Size M, you will likely need a Size L.
  • Due to fabric variation, please allow for a variance of up to 1–3 cm.
